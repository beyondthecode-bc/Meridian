# Privacy Policy for Meridian

Effective date: 2026-08-18

Meridian stores team, preference, and widget data locally on the user's Mac.

Calendar access is optional and is used only to display busy times and create
events at the user's request. Notification access is optional and is used only
for local reminders.

The App Store edition has no analytics, advertising, account system, licensing
service, or developer-operated network service. Meridian does not automatically
send team or Calendar data to the developer.

User-initiated meeting actions may pass selected details to another app or
service:

- Calendar and `.ics` exports can include the meeting time plus participant
  names, cities, and email addresses entered by the user.
- Teams and Google Calendar actions open the selected service with the meeting
  title, time, and participant email addresses entered by the user.

These actions occur only when the user selects them. The destination app or
service processes the information under its own privacy terms.

Exported team archives remain under the user's control. Users delete Meridian
data by using Reset All Data and may remove the app's container through macOS.

Privacy questions: visit https://www.beyondthecode.app/meridian/support or email
hello@beyondthecode.app.
