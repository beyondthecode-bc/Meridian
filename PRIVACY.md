# Privacy Policy for Meridian

Effective date: 2026-07-28

Meridian stores team, preference, and widget data locally on the user's Mac.

Calendar access is optional and is used only to display busy times and create
events at the user's request. Notification access is optional and is used only
for local reminders.

The App Store edition has no analytics, advertising, account system, licensing
service, or developer-operated network service. The app does not collect,
transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Exported team archives remain under the user's control. Users delete Meridian
data by using Reset All Data and may remove the app's container through macOS.

Privacy questions: open an issue at
https://github.com/beyondthecode-bc/Meridian/issues.
